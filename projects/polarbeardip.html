Progressive Web App · World Vision Canada

Polar Bear Dip Event PWA

A mobile event app nobody has to download from an app store. It works offline and includes interactive maps, mini polls and surveys, photo galleries, schedules, a video player and event location information.

Year
Nov 2019
Context
World Vision Canada
Role
Research & development
Stack
JavaScript · PWA · Materialize

Why a progressive web app

World Vision Canada runs many events every year and needed an app to promote and introduce them. With my team, I researched whether to build a progressive web app or a native mobile app.

We concluded a PWA was the better fit: most people don't want to download an app for a single event each year, while a PWA can be added to the home screen in one tap and still looks and feels like a native app.
