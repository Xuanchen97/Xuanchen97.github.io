Featured · In progress 2026 — Present

Site Payments Platform

Our team is replacing a VB6 system that has calculated clinical-trial site payments for over 20 years. The new platform models each site contract as versioned data — payable items, payment criteria, parties, fee schedules and vendors — so studies from DataFax, TrialMaster and REDCap are priced and paid the same way, with a point-in-time history of every agreement.
